How a placement runs

One request in. One recommendation out. You never chase an insurer.

1 · You tell us the risk

One form: the interest, the value, and any adjuster's figure you already have.

2 · We go to market

We email underwriters a structured request. Each insurer gets a private response link — no login, no attachment.

3 · Insurers reply

Premium, excess, exclusions, subjectivities and their own assessed value land straight in your file.

4 · We sort it properly

Price, excess, breadth, claims record and adequacy against the adjuster's value — 100 points, ranked.

5 · You get our report

A ranked recommendation issued by Alfam as your broker, with the exclusions spelled out.

6 · We stay with it

Renewals, endorsements and claims run through the same desk that placed the cover.
